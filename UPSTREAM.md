# Upstream source

Baltboard 2.x is based on AnySoftKeyboard:

- Repository: https://github.com/AnySoftKeyboard/AnySoftKeyboard
- Pinned commit: `dcc654c849fdc773d352a13e82b60634e753e1ec`
- License: Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`)

The upstream engine supplies touch tracking, multi-touch handling, suggestions, gesture typing,
clipboard history, emoji support, input-method lifecycle handling, and system-window-inset support.
Baltboard adds its Lithuanian/English product configuration, custom layouts and themes,
toolbar, clipboard and emoji panels, AI rewrite and translation workflows,
and upgrade-compatible Android component name.
